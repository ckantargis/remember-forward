The Pictorial Lexicon Index

Every word in the lexicon, placed on a plate. Built on the Oxford–Duden pictorial model — 28,000 entries across 384 plates — but run deliberately lighter, at about 55 words per plate instead of 73, so that every word stays readable by the naked eye. No magnifier anywhere in this set. Numbered callouts point into the picture; the words themselves live in a margin strip that can be re-etched per language while the drawing stays untouched.
